# POL-01 — Rules of Engagement (Simulated)

*Simulated training policy for exercise play only. Not real ROE. This platform contains no engagement mechanics.*

## 1. Scope

These rules govern how simulated units respond to contacts and threats within DOIP exercises. The platform simulates **defensive and support operations only**; offensive strike planning is outside scope by design and cannot be scripted.

## 2. Standing rules

1. Observation and reporting are always authorized (SOP-01).
2. Units hold position and observe on contact; they do not pursue.
3. Defensive posture changes (SOP-07 Green/Amber/Red) require operator decision, logged with reason.
4. Escalation beyond posture change — QRF deployment, airspace closure recommendation, sector lockdown — requires the role authority defined in REF-04.
5. No simulated unit acts autonomously against a contact: every response is a human decision, recorded as a decision event.

## 3. Air tracks

Counter-UAS responses follow SOP-05's response ladder and stop at tracking, alerting, and defensive posture. Engagement is not simulated.

## 4. Exercise conduct

Red-Team injects must comply with the fairness constraints in the inject playbook and are always disclosed in the after-action report. Attempting to script actions outside the DSL whitelist is a validation error, not a gray area.
