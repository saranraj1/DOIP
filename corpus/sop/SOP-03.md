# SOP-03 — Casualty Evacuation (CASEVAC)

_Simulated training document. Not real doctrine._

## 1. Initiation

A CASEVAC request is initiated when personnel vitals cross critical thresholds (REF-02 medical bands) or a unit transmits a casualty report. The request includes: casualty count, precedence, location, and security status of the pickup site.

## 2. Precedence categories

- **Urgent** — evacuation within 60 sim-minutes; vitals critical.
- **Priority** — within 4 sim-hours; vitals degraded and worsening.
- **Routine** — within 24 sim-hours; stable.

## 3. Evacuation chain

Casualties move: point of injury → casualty collection point → medical facility. The dispatching operator designates the evacuation asset; UAV assets may relay but never transport. Escort requirements follow SOP-04 when the route crosses an active-threat sector.

## 4. In-transit reporting

The evacuation asset reports at pickup, at each waypoint, and at handover. Vitals telemetry for the casualty is streamed where equipment allows and attached to the run log.

## 5. Records

Every CASEVAC generates a timeline entry for the after-action report: request time, dispatch time, pickup time, handover time. The urgent-precedence 60-minute standard is a primary exercise KPI.
