# SOP-04 — Convoy Escort Procedures

_Simulated training document. Not real doctrine._

## 1. Composition

A standard escorted convoy comprises a lead escort element, cargo vehicles, and a trail escort element. Convoys of more than six cargo vehicles add a middle escort. UAV overwatch is assigned when airframe availability and weather permit.

## 2. Route planning

Routes are planned in the mission planner with threat zones and weather as mandatory cost layers. The optimized route is compared against the naive route; the planner must acknowledge the difference before launch. Restricted zones are never routed through, regardless of cost savings.

## 3. Movement discipline

Convoy speed is the slowest vehicle's standard. Spacing: 50–100 m between vehicles, doubled in reduced visibility. Halt reports at every scheduled waypoint.

## 4. Incident response

On obstruction or contact: the convoy halts short, reports SALUTE (SOP-01), and holds. Escorts secure the immediate area; they do not pursue. Rerouting requires operator approval. If casualties occur, SOP-03 governs.

## 5. Delay thresholds

A convoy more than 20 sim-minutes behind schedule reports cause; more than 45 minutes triggers an escalation-matrix review (REF-04) for QRF readiness along the remaining route.
