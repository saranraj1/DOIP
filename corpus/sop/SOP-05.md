# SOP-05 — Counter-UAS Response

*Simulated training document. Not real doctrine.*

## 1. Detection and classification

Any unidentified air track is classified within 3 sim-minutes as: **friendly** (transponder/flight-plan match), **unknown**, or **hostile-behavior** (loitering over restricted zones, shadowing units, or repeated perimeter approaches). Vision-service detections below the confidence threshold route to human review before classification.

## 2. Alerting

Unknown tracks inside a monitoring zone raise severity **medium**; inside a restricted zone, **high**; hostile-behavior classification is always **critical** and notifies the commander role directly.

## 3. Response ladder

1. Track and record (all classifications).
2. Ground own UAV traffic in the affected sector (unknown in restricted zone or above).
3. Alert ground units along the track's path; sensitive activities pause.
4. Hostile-behavior: continuous tracking priority, QRF readiness per SOP-02, and airspace-closure recommendation to the commander.

This platform simulates detection, tracking, and defensive posture only — no engagement mechanics exist.

## 4. Stand-down

A track that exits the operational area and does not return within 30 sim-minutes is downgraded and archived. All track histories are preserved for after-action review.
