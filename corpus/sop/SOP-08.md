# SOP-08 — HADR Deployment

*Simulated training document. Not real doctrine.*

HADR is a military mission class: the same units, logistics chains, and command procedures as other operations, applied to disaster response (see scenario SC-8).

## 1. Activation

HADR deployment activates on tasking from the exercise authority. Initial actions: designate the operational area, establish the supply chain, and assign sector responsibilities within 60 sim-minutes of activation.

## 2. Priorities

Fixed order unless the commander overrides with logged reason: (1) life-saving movement and medical response (SOP-03 applies unchanged), (2) route clearance for relief movement, (3) supply distribution, (4) infrastructure assessment reporting.

## 3. Logistics tempo

HADR consumes supplies faster than sustainment operations: rations and medical stock are re-forecast every 2 sim-hours, and the resupply threshold rises from 30% to 50% stock. Convoy movements follow SOP-04 with weather as the dominant route cost.

## 4. Coordination and reporting

Civilian-agency liaison is represented as report events only — no civilian units are simulated under command. Situation reports follow the standard cadence at doubled frequency during the first 12 sim-hours. All KPIs (response time, supply coverage, medical timelines) feed the after-action report and the Ch. 14 benchmark comparisons.
