# SOP-02 — Quick Reaction Force (QRF) Dispatch

_Simulated training document. Not real doctrine._

## 1. Dispatch criteria

QRF dispatch is authorized when any of the following hold: (a) a contact report of severity **high** or **critical** (REF-02); (b) a unit under SOP-06 comms-loss escalation at stage 3; (c) a perimeter alert under SOP-07 confirmed by two independent sensors.

## 2. Authorization

Dispatch requires operator action; the system may recommend but never auto-dispatches. The dispatch decision, its timestamp, and the authorizing role are logged as decision events.

## 3. Response standards

QRF acknowledges tasking within 2 sim-minutes and departs within 5. Target time-on-scene is 20 sim-minutes for in-sector responses; cross-sector responses require commander notification. En-route reporting every 5 sim-minutes.

## 4. Feasibility

Before dispatch, verify: fuel above 30% for the round trip, medical kit aboard for casualty-related calls, and route feasibility under current weather (see SOP-04 route rules). If the primary QRF fails feasibility checks, the escalation matrix (REF-04) designates the alternate.

## 5. Stand-down

QRF returns to ready state only on operator stand-down order, logged with reason.
