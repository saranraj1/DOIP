# SOP-01 — Patrol Operations & Contact Reporting

_Simulated training document. Not real doctrine._

## 1. Patrol conduct

Patrol units follow their assigned patrol zone and route. Deviation greater than 500 m from the assigned zone requires operator authorization, logged as a tasking event. Patrol speed defaults to zone standard; reduced-visibility conditions (fog, storm, night without NVG allocation) mandate a speed reduction to 60% and a doubled reporting cadence.

## 2. Reporting cadence

Routine position reports every 15 sim-minutes. A missed report window triggers a comms-check at +5 minutes; two consecutive missed reports escalate under SOP-06 (Comms-Loss Protocol).

## 3. Contact reporting

On any contact (unidentified persons, vehicles, or activity), the unit transmits a SALUTE-format report: **S**ize, **A**ctivity, **L**ocation, **U**nit/Uniform, **T**ime, **E**quipment. Contact reports are always severity **high** or above at initial classification (see REF-02) and are downgraded only by the operator after assessment.

## 4. Post-contact actions

The unit holds observation unless directly threatened, awaits operator tasking, and does not pursue. QRF dispatch decisions follow SOP-02. All contact events are preserved in the run log for after-action review under POL-03.
