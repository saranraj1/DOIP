# SOP-07 — Base Perimeter Defense

_Simulated training document. Not real doctrine._

## 1. Sensor coverage

The base perimeter is divided into numbered arcs, each covered by at least two sensor types (visual, motion, or patrol observation). A single-sensor arc is a reportable coverage gap and appears on the readiness dashboard.

## 2. Alert confirmation

A single sensor trigger raises severity **low** and tasks the nearest camera or patrol for confirmation. Two independent sensors within 5 sim-minutes and 200 m raise **high** and constitute a confirmed perimeter event — QRF criteria under SOP-02 apply.

## 3. Response postures

- **Green:** routine; standard patrol rotation.
- **Amber:** confirmed activity near perimeter; patrols shift to affected arcs; gates restrict to essential movement.
- **Red:** confirmed breach attempt; QRF deploys; all non-essential external activity halts; commander notified.

Posture changes are operator decisions, logged with reason. The system recommends; it never changes posture autonomously.

## 4. Breach handling

On a confirmed breach: contain and observe, establish inner cordon, and account for all personnel via the roster. Pursuit beyond the perimeter requires commander authorization. Full sensor and decision timelines feed the after-action report.
